Research Question Answer
------------------------
The implemented workflow converts analytically suspicious transactions into structured forensic cases by using each suspicious transaction as an investigation seed, linking related transactional evidence through account relationships within a defined temporal window, and recording the resulting evidence and findings with source transaction references. On the PaySim dataset, the final configuration produced 480 candidate cases with 100.00% structural case completeness and 100.00% seed traceability, 100.00% evidence traceability and 100.00% finding traceability. However, only 11 cases (2.29%) received additional contextual evidence, showing that the available transactional dataset and reconstruction rules provide strong source traceability but limited contextual enrichment.

Important Interpretation
------------------------
The results demonstrate a reproducible method for converting
analytical fraud-detection outputs into structured,
evidence-linked transactional forensic cases.

The results do not demonstrate complete real-world forensic
investigation because PaySim does not contain external digital
artifacts. The low contextual enrichment rate is therefore
reported as an empirical limitation of the current dataset and
reconstruction configuration rather than being interpreted as
evidence that real-world investigations would have similarly
limited context.
